Gradient Descent Golf

Five holes, one club: the learning rate. Pick it wisely — too small and you'll be putting forever, too large and the ball flies off the green. Lowest total strokes wins.

🌱 Beginner ~3 min Concept: Gradient Descent
Hole 1 of 5
Drag the slider to set your learning rate η, then putt.
📌 How scoring works
Each hole has a par — the strokes an expert would take with a well-tuned learning rate. Converge fast without overshooting to beat par. Fly off the edge of the green and it's a 5-stroke penalty. Wander forever with too small a step and you'll run out the clock.
